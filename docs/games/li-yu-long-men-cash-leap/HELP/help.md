## 02 HOLD & SPIN FEATURE

Whenever one or more of 🟣, 🟢 or 🟡 appear, the HOLD & SPIN FEATURE may be awarded.

At the beginning of the HOLD & SPIN FEATURE, all triggering Koi symbol(s) activate their respective Koi Streams, display prize values and are added to the Koi Stream, beginning from the top of the reel.

Whenever a 🟢 is added to a collection stream, the prize values displayed on all Koi symbol(s) above it in the Koi Stream are multiplied by 2.
Whenever a 🟡 is added to a collection stream, the prize values displayed on all Koi symbol(s) above it in the Koi stream are multiplied by 3.

If a new Koi symbol(s) would be added to a Koi Stream already containing 3 Koi symbols, the topmost Koi symbol is removed first and its value added to the collection meter above the Koi Streams, then the added Koi symbol(s) modifies the values of the other Koi symbols still in the Koi Stream.

At the beginning of the HOLD & SPIN FEATURE, 3 free spins are awarded.
If one or more 🪙, 🟣, 🟢 or 🟡 appear, the number of free spins remaining is reset to 3.
Whenever 🪙 appears on screen, it is held in its position for the remainder of the HOLD & SPIN FEATURE.
Whenever 🟣, 🟢 or 🟡 appears in an active column, it is added to the Koi Stream for that column.
Whenever 🟣, 🟢 or 🟡 appear in an inactive column, they may activate their respective Koi Streams.

## 03 HOLD & SPIN FEATURE (CONTINUED)

Possible 🪙 prizes are 20 to 750 credits, MINI Jackpot, MINOR Jackpot or MAJOR Jackpot.
Possible Koi symbol credit prizes are 28 to 8888 credits.
🪙 and Koi symbol credit prizes are multiplied by the bet multiplier.
🪙 may also reveal MAJOR Reset Prize in credits, and this is not multiplied by the bet multiplier.
MINI and MINOR Jackpots are multiplied by the bet multiplier.

The HOLD & SPIN FEATURE ends when either no FREE SPINS remain or all positions on reels contain 🪙. At the end of the HOLD & SPIN FEATURE, if all positions on reels contain 🪙, the Grand Jackpot is awarded.

At the end of the HOLD & SPIN FEATURE, the prize values of all 🪙 appearing on reels and Koi symbol(s) appearing on Koi Streams are added to the collection meter. Then, the value of the collection meter is awarded.

Paytable prizes are not awarded during the HOLD & SPIN FEATURE.
Bonus Reels are in play during the HOLD & SPIN FEATURE.
Credits bet is the same as the game that triggered the HOLD & SPIN FEATURE.

## 04 SUPER KOI

Whenever 🌈 appear, the HOLD & SPIN FEATURE is awarded.
The Koi Stream for the column in which the 🌈 appears is activated, and any or all of the other Koi Streams may also be activated.
🌈 displays a prize value and is added to the Koi Stream above the column in which the 🌈 landed.
🌈 is added to the Koi Stream before any other symbol.
Once 🌈 has been added to the Koi Stream, the HOLD & SPIN FEATURE continues as normal.
Possible 🌈 prizes are 28 to 8888 credits.
🌈 credit prizes are multiplied by the bet multiplier.
🌈 can appear on any reel.
At most 1 🌈 can appear on reels per base game spin.
🌈 cannot appear during the HOLD & SPIN FEATURE.
🌈 is only available at whole number multiples of the 100cr bet.
Credits bet is the same as the game that triggered the HOLD & SPIN FEATURE.

## 06 GRAND JACKPOT／MAJOR JACKPOT

**GRAND JACKPOT**
GRAND JACKPOT is awarded when 🪙 are collected on all available positions at the end of the HOLD & SPIN FEATURE.
All 🪙 prizes appearing on reels and all Koi symbol prizes appearing on Koi Streams are paid in addition to GRAND JACKPOT.
There is a chance that GRAND JACKPOT may also be awarded after a base game spin which does not trigger the HOLD & SPIN FEATURE.
The odds of the GRAND JACKPOT in base game change with the bet, bet multiplier and denomination.
The odds of the GRAND JACKPOT in HOLD & SPIN FEATURE change with the bet structure.
Maximum Grand JACKPOT Meter amount is $3,999,999.99 with further contributions added to subsequent progressives after the ceiling has been reached.

**MAJOR JACKPOT**
MAJOR JACKPOT is awarded when MAJOR appears on a 🪙 at the end of the HOLD & SPIN FEATURE.
GRAND JACKPOT and MAJOR JACKPOT are progressive.
MINOR and MINI are Jackpot Bonus Prizes.
MINI Jackpot is 1000 credits times bet multiplier.
MINOR Jackpot is 2500 credits times bet multiplier.
Jackpot Progressives are not multiplied by the bet multiplier.
The odds of Credit Prizes and Jackpot Prizes change with total credits bet.
The odds of the MAJOR JACKPOT change with the bet, bet multiplier and denomination.
Progressive meter amount is reset each time it is paid, before any other progressives are paid.
When a GRAND or MAJOR JACKPOT is won for any bet, that level is adjusted for all bets.
Maximum Major JACKPOT Meter amount is $3,999,999.99 with further contributions added to subsequent progressives after the ceiling has been reached.
